# ImageGen 提示词

工具使用内置 ImageGen。三张概念图分别生成。

## a-cue-tag

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished wide 16:9 in-combat concept screenshot for a Revue Starlight inspired indie fantasy deckbuilder mod, a visual design proposal for a persistent Call and Response power. Original conceptual HUD styling, no logo. Dark muted blue violet theatrical stone arena, very clear visual hierarchy. At 27% width an anime young adult female archer with short dark purple hair, round glasses, violet/navy stage uniform and bow, full body at realistic small combat sprite scale. At 76% width a subdued shadow enemy; wide quiet negative space between both. Health and simple existing status icons are small and visually subdued. At the bottom an ordered five-card hand, anchor the cards to the bottom margin, with 2nd and 4th cards illustrated as distinct ordinary arrow-category fantasy cards, 1st an open book, 3rd a sword, 5th a stage star. Show an arrow-phase snapshot. Clear small-screen functionality; effect uses modest localized brightness, minimal particles, no giant signage, no editorial layout, no captions or headings outside game UI, no extra faces. UI text requested verbatim in Simplified Chinese. Do not invent long card text.
A — Character-side cue tag. Foreground design focus is a small rounded dark slate plaque anchored beside the archer's shoulder, sized around 15% of total image width. A thin split reverberating curved echo line in blue and a solid simple diagonal arrow glyph on the left, text on the right. Render exactly two lines: “下次触发” as the small line and “飞矢牌” in larger ivory type. Airy pair of tiny echo arcs next to the plaque refer to call and response, no floor light and no repeating HUD strip. Mark both the second and fourth cards with the same small icy blue corner chevron badge; avoid full borders. All other cards look normal. Make the text clear and keep the plaque on screen in the battle composition. No other interface text unless a few HP digits. Clean sharp edges, not high contrast everywhere.
```
